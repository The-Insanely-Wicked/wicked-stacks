# Delivery Emails — branded, automatic, one per product

These are the emails buyers get the moment they purchase. Set up once in
MailerLite, runs forever.

## How the plumbing works (plain English)

1. In **MailerLite → Integrations → Stripe → Connect**, log into your
   Stripe account. Now MailerLite hears about every purchase.
2. Make one **Automation** per product: trigger = "purchases a product
   via Stripe" → pick the product → action = send the matching email
   below.
3. That's it. Customer buys → Stripe tells MailerLite → MailerLite sends
   the branded email with the download links → the buyer is also on your
   list forever (put them in a "Customers" group in the same automation).

Replace every `[LINK]` with the Drive/Dropbox share link for that file
before turning each automation on. Send a $1 test to yourself first
(make a 99%-off code in Stripe, buy your own product, check the email).

**Subject lines and body are written in Michael's voice — paste as-is.**
