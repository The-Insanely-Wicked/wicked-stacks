## Launch Email 2 — Day 2
**Subject:** Why there's a kids' book about a piggy bank next to a business book

Someone's going to look at Wicked Stacks and ask why a slightly sarcastic
business book shares a shelf with a princess who throws problem-solving
parties.

Fair question. Here's the answer: it's all the same project.

Complete Business Mastery teaches adults how money and businesses actually
work. Penny's Piggy Bank teaches a five-year-old the same thing at
five-year-old scale — save, share, choose. Stuck No More gets a person
moving again; Princess Penny teaches a kid to break a big problem into
small steps. Same ideas, different sized humans.

I write about the stuff I wish someone had taught me earlier. Some of it
comes out with spreadsheets, some of it comes out with a piggy bank.

**[The whole family's covered → [STORE LINK]]**

— Michael
