## Email 2 — Day 1
**Subject:** The one mistake in that guide almost everyone makes

Quick follow-up on the Video Marketing Quickstart.

The #1 place people stall out isn't the software, the script, or the voice-
over. It's this: they try to make one *perfect* video instead of five
honest ones.

Perfect is a trap. Your fifth video will embarrass your first — that's not
failure, that's the process working. The businesses winning with video
right now are not the most talented; they're the most consistent.

So here's the assignment, if you want it: this week, make one genuinely
mediocre video. On purpose. Post it. You'll learn more from that than from
another month of research.

book nobody asked for.
