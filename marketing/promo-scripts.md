# Promo Scripts — Talking-Head Videos, Image Posts & Announcements

Ready-to-read scripts in the house voice. Each talking-head script runs 45–75
seconds read naturally. [Brackets] = say it your way. Every video: hook in the
first 3 seconds, captions on, one soft ask at the end.

## Image-post ideas (Pinterest / Instagram / Facebook)

1. **Quote cards** — one sharp line from each book on the brand gradient.
   ("Research mode is fear dressed up as preparation." — From Zero)
2. **"What's included" graphic** — one book cover + icons for PDF/EPUB/
   workbook/audio + "one price." The whole store pitch in one image.
3. **Penny page peeks** — a single illustrated spread + "read it, then
   listen — audiobook included."
4. **Before/after** — "Buying an ebook anywhere else" (nickel-and-dime list)
   vs "Buying at Wicked Stacks" (everything included).
5. **The stack shot** — all six covers in a row + "The Everything Stack, $79."
6. **DoodleAI frame-grabs** — a doodle mid-draw + "AI drew this. From my
   script. In minutes."
7. **The 30-second formula** — hook/value/ask as a simple 3-step graphic
   (pulls people to the free guide).
8. **Kids + money tips** — "3 things Penny teaches" carousel for parent
   groups and Pinterest.
9. **Behind-the-scenes** — screenshot of the audiobook exporting at 58% +
   "the promise ships tonight."
10. **The bolt** — brand mark + one-liner, posted occasionally so feeds
    learn the logo.
