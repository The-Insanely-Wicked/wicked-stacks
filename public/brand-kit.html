Voice — how the store sounds

Bios — copy you can paste

Short: Wicked Stacks™ is the bookstore of The Insanely Wicked — real books written by a real person. Business guides with actual worksheets, honest self-help, and illustrated kids' stories, each sold with every format included and delivered instantly. Stack your shelf. Stack your skills.
Full: Wicked Stacks™ is an independent online bookstore with a simple rule: when you buy a book, you get the whole book — PDF, e-reader edition, workbooks, audiobooks — one price, delivered the moment you pay, with future format upgrades added to your library free. The shelf ranges from Complete Business Mastery (a year and a half in the making, with a full toolkit of templates) to Stuck No More's honest self-help to the illustrated Penny series for kids. Every title is written by Michael Gardner, the builder behind The Insanely Wicked. No subscriptions, no locked apps — you buy a book, you own the book.
