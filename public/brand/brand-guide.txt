MISSION
-------
Real books written by a real person, sold the way we wish stores sold
them: every format you need — ebook, workbook, audio — for one fair
price, delivered the second you pay, with future format upgrades free.
No subscriptions, no locked apps, no nickel-and-diming. You buy a book,
you own the book.

BRAND VOICE — HOW THE STORE SOUNDS
----------------------------------
Wicked Stacks shares The Insanely Wicked's DNA (plain beats fancy, funny
never mean, no guru talk ever) with two store-specific twists:

1. A little more playful. We sell kids' books about a piggy bank next to
   business books — the shelf has range, and the voice can smile.
2. We talk like a bookseller, not a marketer. "You might like this
   because..." beats "UNLOCK THE SECRETS OF..."
3. Every claim is checkable. Page counts, what's included, what format —
   exact, honest, no padding.
4. The customer is smart. We never write down to anyone — including the
   four-year-olds. Especially the four-year-olds.

LOGO RULES
----------
- The mark is the interlocked-blocks "S" (transparent PNG). Never
  stretch, recolor, rotate, or add effects.
- The wordmark is "WickedStacks™" — "Wicked" in off-white, "Stacks" in
  the signature gradient, TM small and dim.
- The mark alone works for avatars, favicons, and app icons.
- Clear space around the mark: at least 1/4 of its width on all sides.
- ™ (not ℠) — Wicked Stacks is a product brand, pending registration.
