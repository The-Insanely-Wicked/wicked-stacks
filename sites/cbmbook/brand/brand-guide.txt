WHAT THIS SITE IS
-----------------
cbmbook.com is the standalone home of one book: Complete Business
Mastery. The same book is also sold on wickedstacks.com, through the
same checkout link, at the same price. This site exists to rank and
convert for people searching for a business book, not to be a second
store.

Because it is one product on one page, the site has one job: explain
what is in the book honestly enough that the right person buys it and
the wrong person doesn't.

VISUAL RELATIONSHIP TO THE STORE
--------------------------------
CBM Book is a child of Wicked Stacks and looks like it. Same palette,
same fonts, same gradient. Someone landing here from the store should
feel they never left the family.

What is different: this site is quieter. No category nav, no shelf, no
cross-sell. One book, one price, one button.

BRAND VOICE
-----------
Shares The Insanely Wicked's DNA (plain beats fancy, funny never mean,
no guru talk ever), plus the book's own register:

1. Slightly sarcastic, never cynical. The book calls out nonsense; it
   does not sneer at the reader for having believed it.
2. Numbers over adjectives. "17 chapters, roughly 130 pages" beats
   "comprehensive". Every claim on this page is checkable.
3. Anti-guru by construction. No secrets, no hacks, no "what nobody
   tells you". The book's whole argument is that the boring math is
   the thing nobody does.
4. Honest about who it is not for. A page that repels the wrong buyer
   earns more than one that converts everyone and refunds half.

CONTENT RULES — NOT NEGOTIABLE
------------------------------
- Never invent statistics, studies, expert names, or testimonials.
  There are no reviews on this page because there are no real reviews
  to quote yet.
- Never invent author credentials. The author is a founder who wrote
  what he learned. That is the claim, and it is the whole claim.
- Case studies in the book use anonymised real people. Keep the
  anonymisation in marketing too — never swap in a real name.
- Page counts, chapter counts, runtimes and formats must match what
  the buyer actually receives. Check src/products.ts before changing
  any of them here.
