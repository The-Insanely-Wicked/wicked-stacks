What this site is

cbmbook.com is the standalone home of one book. The same book is also sold on wickedstacks.com, through the same checkout link, at the same price. This site exists to rank and convert for people searching for a business book — not to be a second store.

One product, one page, one job: explain what's in the book honestly enough that the right person buys it and the wrong person doesn't.

Relationship to the store

CBM Book is a child of Wicked Stacks and looks like it — same palette, same fonts, same gradient. Someone arriving from the store should feel they never left the family.

What's different: this site is quieter. No category nav, no shelf, no cross-sell. One book, one price, one button.

Voice

  • Slightly sarcastic, never cynical. The book calls out nonsense; it doesn't sneer at the reader for having believed it.
  • Numbers over adjectives. "17 chapters, roughly 130 pages" beats "comprehensive".
  • Anti-guru by construction. No secrets, no hacks, no "what nobody tells you". The book's argument is that the boring math is the thing nobody does.
  • Honest about who it isn't for. A page that repels the wrong buyer earns more than one that converts everyone and refunds half.

Taglines

Primary: The ultimate (and slightly sarcastic) guide to building, running, and growing any business.
Secondary: Real frameworks. Real numbers. Zero guru fluff.
Format: Every format included. Future upgrades free.

Bios — copy you can paste

Short (~40 words)

Complete Business Mastery is a 17-chapter guide to building, running, and growing a business — unit economics, market sizing, sales systems, hiring, and automation, with the math shown. Includes a full Resource Pack of templates, plus hours of audio and video.

Full (~95 words)

Complete Business Mastery is the flagship title from Wicked Stacks: 17 chapters covering the whole life of a business, from mindset and unit economics through market research, branding, sales systems, operations, hiring, automation, and scaling. It took a year and a half to write because it does not skip the hard parts — how to size a market three ways, when automation actually pays for itself, and why the butcher always gets paid. Every copy includes the Resource Pack of fill-in-the-blank templates and scripts, seven audio deep-dives, and three videos.

Content rules — not negotiable

  • Never invent statistics, studies, expert names, or testimonials. There are no reviews on this page because there are no real ones to quote yet.
  • Never invent author credentials. The author is a founder who wrote what he learned. That's the claim, and it's the whole claim.
  • Case studies use anonymised real people. Keep the anonymisation in marketing too — never swap in a real name.
  • Counts and formats must match what the buyer receives. Check src/products.ts before changing any of them here.
