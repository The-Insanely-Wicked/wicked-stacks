Doodle Video Scripts That Sell: The 60-Second Formula

DoodleAI Blog · Scripts

The software draws the video. The script decides whether anyone buys. Sixty seconds is roughly 160 spoken words — here's how to spend every one of them.

The six beats

  • 0–5s — The hook. Their problem, in their words. “Still doing invoices by hand?” beats any clever opener.
  • 5–15s — The twist. Why the problem is worse than they thought: the hours, the cost, the compounding annoyance.
  • 15–30s — The reveal. Introduce the fix. One sentence on what it is; the drawing carries the rest.
  • 30–45s — The proof. How it works in three steps, or one concrete result. Specifics persuade; adjectives don't.
  • 45–55s — The objection. Kill the one doubt you know they have: price, difficulty, or “will this work for me?”
  • 55–60s — The ask. One action, stated plainly. “Click the button below” outperforms poetry.

The three retention killers

Starting with your company name (nobody cares yet). Two calls to action (a confused viewer picks neither). And sentences you can't say in one breath — if you can't breathe it, they can't follow it.
